HOMEPAGE HERO SLIDERS — drop 4 files here
=========================================
  exterior-before.jpg
  exterior-after.jpg
  interior-before.jpg
  interior-after.jpg

.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp and .avif all work.

A slider only turns on when BOTH halves of its pair are present. Add just
exterior-before.jpg and the Exterior slot stays a placeholder until
exterior-after.jpg lands too.

Shoot each pair at the SAME orientation and aspect ratio, or the two halves
drift apart as the slider is dragged.
