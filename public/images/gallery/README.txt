GALLERY — drop up to 20 single photos here
==========================================
No pairs, no fixed names. Every image in this folder is picked up
automatically, sorted by filename, up to 20.

Captions are generated from the filename:
  toyota-camry-full-detail.jpg   ->  Full Detail — Toyota Camry
  gmc-sierra-exterior.jpg        ->  Exterior Detail — GMC Sierra
  lincoln-navigator-interior.jpg ->  Interior Detail — Lincoln Navigator
  IMG_4821.jpg                   ->  Recent Detail — Lakeland, FL

Pattern: <make>-<model>-<service>.jpg  (all lowercase, hyphens between words)
Services understood: full detail, exterior, interior, interior deep clean,
maintenance, ceramic/protection coating.

To control the order, prefix with numbers: 01-..., 02-... The prefix is
stripped from the caption.

The words "before" and "after" are ignored in filenames and never appear in a
caption — these are result photos, not comparison pairs.
